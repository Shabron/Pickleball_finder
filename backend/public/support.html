Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Download the Pickleball Finder app, tap Sign Up on the welcome screen, and follow the steps to create your profile with your name, email, skill level, and location.

How do I find pickleball partners near me?

After logging in, use the Find Players section to browse players in your area. You can filter by skill level, availability, and distance.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap Forgot Password and enter your email address. You'll receive instructions to reset your password.

How do I delete my account?

To request account deletion, please email us at shauryamspp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account" and we will process your request within 7 business days.

The app is not working correctly. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, ensure you have the latest version installed from the App Store or Google Play. If the problem continues, please contact us with a description of the issue.
